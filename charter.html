Mission Statement

The Anatolia Hardware Club (AHC) is an independent youth organization based in Ankara, established to enable young people to freely develop their theoretical and practical skills in the fields of hardware development, embedded systems, robotics, DIY electronics, and open-source software. Operating as a completely independent civil youth movement, it is not affiliated with any educational or political institution.

CHAPTER I: GENERAL RULES

Article 1: Club Name and Initials

The name of the youth organization established under these bylaws is “Anatolia Hardware Club,” and its abbreviation is “AHC.” It will hereinafter be referred to as “AHC.”

Article 2 – Structure and Independence

AHC is a completely independent regional youth community that is not affiliated with any specific school, college, or university and operates outside of any hierarchical structure. Decision-making mechanisms and management processes are carried out solely through votes by the founding board and community members.

Article 3 – Financial Responsibility and Sponsorship

AHC utilizes the financial sponsorship infrastructure of Hack Club Bank (HCB), which operates under “The Hack Foundation,” an international technology and education foundation. All of the community’s revenues, donations, and project budgets are managed transparently under the HCB’s structure, guarantee, and oversight.

Section 3/A – Principles of Card and Balance Management

  • Card Ownership and Authority: All usage, password, and management rights for physical and virtual cards issued in the name of AHC are exclusively under the control of the Founding Leaders (Ozan Koçer and Nusret Efe Gürlek). Cards may not be transferred to or shared with third parties.
  • Spending Limits and Approval: All expenditures from the club’s balance for equipment, the website, servers, or events must be approved jointly by two Founding Leaders. For expenditures exceeding any specified amount, written or digital consent is required.
  • Transparency and Invoicing Requirements: An invoice or receipt for every card transaction is transparently uploaded to the Hack Club Bank (HCB) system in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. The club balance may not be used for personal expenses under any circumstances.

CHAPTER II: MEMBERSHIP AND OPERATIONS

Article 4 – Membership Requirements

Any young person interested in technology, hardware, software, manufacturing, and coding can join the community. Membership is open to all, regardless of school, academic performance, race, religion, language, gender, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status. Equal opportunity is a fundamental principle.

Article 5 – Work Procedures and Safety

Adherence to safety guidelines is essential during hardware development processes (such as soldering, 3D printing, microcontroller assembly, etc.). Knowledge sharing within the community is based entirely on the open-source philosophy; schematics and code for completed projects are shared with the world via the GitHub organization under the GNU GPL v3.00 license.

CHAPTER III: BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Article 6 – Management Structure

The community adopts a horizontal management model. The founding leaders are responsible for the community’s administrative coordination, financial management, and external relations. Decisions are made by a two-thirds majority and by consensus.

CHAPTER IV: CHANGES AND ENTRY INTO FORCE

Article 7 - Right to Amend

In line with the evolving and growing needs of the community, the provisions of these bylaws and the community name may be revised by decision of the Founding Board. Name changes on digital platforms (GitHub, HCB, etc.) will be implemented subject to the approval of these bylaws.

Article 8 - Entry into Force

These bylaws consist of a total of 3 pages, including all pages, and 8 articles. They were prepared in electronic form and, as of June 6, 2026, have been read, accepted, and put into effect by the founders of the Anatolia Hardware Club in electronic form.
